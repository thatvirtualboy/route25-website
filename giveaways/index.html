How giveaways work.

Look for the gift icon

When a giveaway is coming soon or open, a gift icon appears at the top of Home. Tap it to see the prize and official rules.

Enter the drawing

Sign in and enter each giveaway you want to join. Having an account doesn’t enter you automatically.

Watch for your result

Winners are selected at random and notified in the app. If you win, claim your prize before the deadline and provide your shipping details.

Good to know

A few things
before you enter.

Each giveaway has its own official rules. Here’s what you can generally expect.

Who can enter?

Giveaways are open to eligible Route 25 users. We’re starting with United States residents ages 18 and older. Check each event’s official rules for the full eligibility requirements and exclusions.

Can I earn extra entries?

When a giveaway offers referral bonuses, invite friends to join the Route 25 community using your personal invitation link or code. A friend must create a new account and complete signup verification before entries close for your bonus to count.

You can earn up to three bonus entries per giveaway. The same opportunity is available to everyone. Friends join for free. See the event’s rules for the details.

What happens if a prize isn’t claimed?

The selected winner has a limited time to claim. If the prize goes unclaimed or the winner isn’t eligible, another winner may be selected according to the event’s rules.

Where are the official rules?

Read the rules on each giveaway’s page in the app before entering. Each published event also has a permanent public rules link that remains available after it ends.

The rules cover the exact prize, dates, eligibility, entry options, selection process, claim deadline, and sponsor details. This page is a general overview; the event’s official rules govern that giveaway.
