Your Pokémon TCG adventure is evolving.

Welcome to Route 25, the first social app built exclusively for Pokémon TCG collectors. Share, trade, track your collection, and level up your journey in a safe, modern, trainer-powered community.

Fully native iOS with Live Activities, Siri & Shortcuts, and Liquid Glass—made to feel right at home on iPhone.

Every feature built for collectors.

We're on a mission to make Pokémon TCG social again, built for collectors and powered by the community.

Social feed

Show off pulls, share slabs, follow Trainers.

Post the moments that matter and discover what the community is collecting right now. Participate in Trainer Talk, the daily question answered with cards

  • Feed-first collecting, with a modern look
  • Trainer Talk: replies powered by cards
  • Discover new collectors and creators
Direct trades

Easy, direct trainer-to-trainer trades for serious collectors.

Trade directly with other Trainers in your country. Level gates, trade fees, and Trainer Plus perks keep only serious trainers engaged.

  • Direct trainer-to-trainer trade flow
  • Country-limited matching, with US + CAN support
  • Level 3+ access helps keep traders serious
Binder

Track your collection. Explore every set.

Build your Binder, browse expansions, customize slabs, and highlight your favorite cards in your Binder Showcase.

  • Binder Showcase for top cards
  • Browse every set ever released
  • Slab customization
Collection value

Know what your collection is worth.

Get valuable insights into your collection's value with trending data, interesting stats, as well as expansion and per-card market values.

  • Estimated collection value in USD
  • Trending data and biggest movers
  • Expansion and per-card market values
Mini games

Trainer Trials: fast, skill-based mini games.

Test your memory, recognition, and collector instincts to earn bonus XP and prove you’ve got what it takes to become a true master.

  • Put your knowledge to the test
  • Quick games designed for short sessions
  • Earn bonus XP
Level up

Earn XP, unlock flair, go further with Trainer Plus.

Participate, collect, and trade to earn experience and in-app customization. Trainer Plus subscribers get trading benefits alongside perks like extra trade slots, reduced brokering fees, a card scanner, and more.

  • Progression that rewards participation
  • Premium customization and exclusive sprites
  • Trading benefits for Trainer Plus subscribers
