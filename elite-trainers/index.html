Partner Program

Elite Trainers

Elite Trainers are Route 25 partners who help promote the app on their social platforms, and engage with the community. In return, we highlight them in the app and link to their preferred channel.

What Elite Trainers get

  • ⭐ Elite Trainer status (name + badge)
  • 🎁 Free Trainer Plus (normally $3.99/mo or $39.99/yr)
  • 👀 Early access to features (via TestFlight)
  • 📣 Featured in-app with social links
  • 💬 Direct feedback line to the Route 25 team
  • 💵 $50 for every 50 verified user sign ups*

What we ask (monthly)

Keep it natural. Elite Trainers should genuinely enjoy using Route 25. We ask for the following minimum activity each month:

  • Post at least 1 trade in Route 25
  • Post at least 3 verified pack pulls in Route 25
  • Engage naturally in Route 25 (likes, follows, reactions, etc.)

On social

  • Share Route 25 at least once per month on your social channels, in a short-form format (e.g. Stories, TikTok, Shorts), in your own style. This could be letting followers know where you post your pulls, showing off a new trade, highlighting a collection milestone, or anything else that feels natural to your content.
  • Keep a Route 25 mention in bio (e.g. “Elite Trainer on @Route25App” or "Route 25 referral link")

Important note

Elite Trainers are independent partners (not employees). This program is invite-only and may change or end at any time. Additionally, Elite Trainer status may be revoked at any time for any reason (though we doubt this will be a common issue 😊).

Interested?

This is a hand-selected program (not open enrollment), but we love meeting new creators. Send your Route 25 username/trainerID (if available) and your channel links (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.) to hello@route25.app and we’ll reach out if it’s a fit.

* Referral tracking note: to make sure we properly capture your referral, new users should first download Route 25, then open your referral link, then complete sign up with a Route 25 account. Benefit subject to change.
